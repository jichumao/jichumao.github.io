CUDA Path Tracer

A GPU path tracer written in CUDA. It loads glTF meshes and materials and simulates light transport for soft shadows, caustics and depth of field, with stream compaction and bounding-box culling to cut the work per frame.

Overview

The highlight renders use 5,000 samples per pixel or more, with up to 8 bounces per ray.

Visual features

  • BSDFsDiffuse, glossy, perfectly specular and refractive
  • Anti-aliasingStochastic sampling
  • Depth of fieldPhysically based, with a thin-lens camera
  • glTF meshesLoaded with texture mapping
  • Environment mapsHDR lighting
  • Tone mappingACES and Reinhard, plus gamma correction

Performance work

  • Stream compactionRemoves finished rays. In open scenes, frame time drops from 25.7 ms to 18.2 ms.
  • Bounding-box culling20–40% faster on low- to mid-poly glTF models
  • Material sortingMeasured too, and turned out slower on small scenes
