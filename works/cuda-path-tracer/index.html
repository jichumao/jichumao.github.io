CUDA Path Tracer

A GPU path tracer written in CUDA. It loads glTF meshes and materials and simulates light transport for soft shadows, caustics and depth of field, with stream compaction and bounding-box culling to cut the work per frame.

Overview

The highlight renders use 5,000 samples per pixel or more, with up to 8 bounces per ray.

Visual features:

  • Diffuse, glossy, perfectly specular and refractive BSDFs
  • Stochastic sampled anti-aliasing
  • Physically-based depth of field with a thin-lens camera
  • glTF mesh loading with texture mapping
  • HDR environment maps
  • ACES and Reinhard tone mapping, gamma correction

Performance work:

  • Stream compaction removes finished rays; in open scenes render time drops from 25.7 ms to 18.2 ms
  • Bounding-box culling for glTF meshes, 20–40% faster on low- to mid-poly models
  • Material sorting was measured too, and turned out slower on small scenes
