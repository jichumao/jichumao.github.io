CUDA Flocking

A flocking simulation in CUDA after Craig Reynolds’ boids model: cohesion, separation and alignment for up to ten million boids, with three neighbour searches compared.

  • 3neighbour-search implementations compared

Overview

  • Three rulesCohesion, separation and alignment
  • Brute forceEvery boid checks every other boid
  • Scattered uniform gridBoids are binned into cells, so only nearby cells are searched
  • Coherent uniform gridBoid data is reordered to follow the cells, which makes memory access contiguous

Finer cells helped more than expected. Above 100,000 boids, a cell width of 1× the rule distance beat 2×, although 27 cells are checked instead of 8: the coherent grid then reaches 791 fps at 1 million boids and 18 fps at 10 million.
