Overview

An implementation of World-Space Spatiotemporal Path Resampling for Path Tracing with Vulkan ray tracing, made together with Zhiyi Zhou.

The renderer can switch between three paths: the ray-tracing pipeline, ray queries in a compute shader, and world-space ReSTIR.

My part

  • World-space hash gridA compound key of position and normal groups samples that are close in space and in orientation. Cells grow with distance from the camera.
  • Temporal and spatial reuseMotion vectors from the camera transform carry last frame’s samples forward. Neighbours come from the hash grid and are checked by angle and depth before they join the reservoir.
  • DenoisingIntel Open Image Denoise on albedo, normal and noisy colour. The round trip through the CPU costs about 250 ms per frame, which the project lists as its main bottleneck.
