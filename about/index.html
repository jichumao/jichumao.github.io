Hi! I’m Jichu! I am really glad that you have come to my website!

I’m a Graphics Performance Engineer at TikTok, where I optimize the real-time effect rendering engine used by hundreds of millions of creators every day.

I graduated from the University of Pennsylvania (UPenn) in May 2026 with a Master of Science in Engineering in Computer Science & Computer Graphics. I specialize in GPU rendering, real-time graphics and advanced rendering techniques, with hands-on experience in CUDA, OpenGL, Vulkan and Unreal Engine.

During my studies in Software Engineering and Computer Graphics, I designed & developed various interesting games and software toolkits.
