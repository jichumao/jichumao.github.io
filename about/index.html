Jichu Mao

I’m a Graphics Performance Engineer at TikTok, where I optimize the real-time effect rendering engine used by hundreds of millions of creators every day.

I graduated from the University of Pennsylvania in May 2026 with a Master of Science in Engineering in Computer Science & Computer Graphics. I specialize in GPU rendering, real-time graphics and advanced rendering techniques, with hands-on experience in CUDA, OpenGL, Vulkan and Unreal Engine.

The projects on this site come from those years: renderers, shaders, tools and games. Away from the GPU I take photographs: landscapes, cities after dark and the odd portrait. See the photographs.
