About

I’m Jichu Mao. I work on real-time rendering and GPU performance, and I photograph on the side: landscapes, cities after dark, and now and then a portrait.

These pictures were made in 2025 and 2026 in California, on the East Coast of the United States and in China, on a Sony α7C II and a Canon EOS 5D Mark IV.
